# The set-completing sale in positions from real games

100 positions, the first qualifying moment of each of 1,263 screened games between four simple bots. Turns 6-163; buyers held £730-£1,585; the bank held 0-22 unowned deeds. 2,000 paired rollouts per price and arm. Prices are multiples of the missing orange's printed price (£180 for Bow Street and Marlborough Street, £200 for Vine Street). Changes in win chance in percentage points; intervals resample whole positions.

| Baseline | Price | Buyer: mean change | Seller: mean change | Both gain | Buyer loses |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| kept for good | 2× face | +30.6 [+26.2, +35.0] | -1.8 [-3.5, -0.2] | 39 / 100 | 9 / 100 |
| kept for good | 3× face | +17.5 [+13.7, +21.3] | +6.3 [+4.6, +8.0] | 73 / 100 | 13 / 100 |
| kept for good | 4× face | +5.3 [+2.0, +8.6] | +15.5 [+13.4, +17.6] | 71 / 100 | 24 / 100 |
| later trades allowed | 2× face | +0.0 [-0.4, +0.5] | +0.1 [-0.1, +0.2] | 7 / 100 | 37 / 100 |
| later trades allowed | 3× face | -13.0 [-14.3, -11.8] | +8.1 [+7.0, +9.2] | 0 / 100 | 100 / 100 |
| later trades allowed | 4× face | -25.2 [-27.4, -23.0] | +17.3 [+15.3, +19.3] | 0 / 100 | 100 / 100 |

Carrying on as normal, the buyer got the missing orange by a later trade in 99% of the keep games on average; across positions the median cash paid was 2.0× face value, and 1% of those deals were swaps (property given in exchange, so the cash is only part of the price). "Both gain" and "buyer loses" count positions by their point estimates.
