How strong are the meta agents?

Meta agents were evolved by self-play, then hardened: challengers trained to beat them joined the opponent pool until none won by 5% or more, or self-play cycled and the least exploitable agent was kept. 1.0 means level with the player it replaces, on the same 4,000 games.
