Where the dice take you

Each tower is as tall as the square's expected landings per 100 turns, from the exact Markov chain (players stay in jail and roll for doubles). Landing odds are the same at every table size. Drag to turn the board; tap a square for its card.

Each tower is as tall as the most rent the square can charge: a street with a hotel, or a station when one player owns all four. Utilities charge by the dice, so they have no tower.

Each tower is as tall as the most the player it completes a set for should pay, at the chosen table size, from paired rollouts against simple bots. Squares with no price that helps the buyer have no tower.

The board lights up where one throw from the pawn's square can take you, cards and Go to Jail included, from the exact chain. Roll the dice to move the pawn; the light follows it.

Champions' table

How strong are the meta agents?

Meta agents were evolved by self-play, then hardened: challengers trained to beat them joined the opponent pool until none won by 5% or more, or self-play cycled and the least exploitable agent was kept. 1.0 means level with the player it replaces, on the same 4,000 games.
